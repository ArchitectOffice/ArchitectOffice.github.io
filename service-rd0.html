RD-0 Rapid Diagnostic

Entry-level system diagnostic for identifying whether a real structural failure exists.

What it is

RD-0 is a structured intake diagnostic used to determine whether a system issue is real, structural, or symptomatic noise.

What it solves

Eliminates uncertainty at the entry point of analysis by identifying whether deeper diagnostic work is required.

How it works

Signal intake → structural triage → failure classification → resolution direction

Outcome

A clear determination of whether a system failure exists and where it is located.

Diagnostic Flow

Intake → Triage → Classification → Direction
