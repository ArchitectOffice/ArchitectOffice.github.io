Engagement Structure

Diagnostic and structured cognition services designed to identify and stabilise AI interaction systems.

Engagement Principle

These services are not task-based consulting sessions.

They are diagnostic interventions designed to identify where structural misalignment exists between human intent and AI system behaviour.

Each tier reflects depth of structural analysis and level of system integration required.

Tier 1 — Decision Structure & Coherence Session

£150 – £250

Problem: Unclear or inconsistent AI outputs and decision confusion.

Mechanism: Rapid diagnostic analysis of input structure, intent clarity, and interaction patterns.

Outcome: Identification of structural breakdown points and clarity on corrective direction.

Tier 2 — AI Cognitive Lens Session

£250 – £500

Problem: AI outputs not reliably matching intended operational use.

Mechanism: Applied restructuring of AI interaction within real workflows and domain context.

Outcome: Stable, repeatable AI behaviour aligned to structured intent.

Tier 3 — Extended Integration & Coherence Mapping

£500+

Problem: Long-term instability in AI-assisted operational systems.

Mechanism: Iterative refinement and system-level integration of structured cognition principles.

Outcome: Durable operational alignment between intent, workflow, and AI system response.

Who This Is For

  • Operators using AI in decision-critical workflows
  • Individuals experiencing inconsistent AI outputs
  • Systems requiring structured interaction design
  • Environments where clarity affects performance

Not For

  • Generic AI coaching
  • Automation implementation services
  • Productivity optimisation frameworks
  • Tool training or tutorials

Engagement is appropriate where system clarity is required, not where surface-level optimisation is sufficient.
