Pricing

Structured diagnostic engagement is not time-based. It is based on system complexity, uncertainty, and structural instability.

Engagement Principle

Pricing reflects the level of structural uncertainty within a system, not duration or effort. Each engagement is a diagnostic process designed to reduce ambiguity and stabilise decision pathways.
