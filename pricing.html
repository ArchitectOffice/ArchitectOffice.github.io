Pricing & Engagement Framework

Structured diagnostic engagement based on system uncertainty and structural failure depth.

Core Pricing Principle

Pricing is not time-based or effort-based. It is based on system uncertainty, structural failure depth, and clarity required to resolve ambiguity.

Pricing functions as a filter for signal quality, not a valuation of labour.

RD-0 Rapid Diagnostic Entry Point

Entry-level system classification layer

Purpose: Determine whether a real system failure exists.

Nature: Short-form diagnostic intake for ambiguity assessment.

Output:

  • Failure / non-failure classification
  • High-level fault localisation
  • Identification of structural instability

Pricing Range: £75–£250

OSD Operational Systems Diagnostic

Deep structural failure analysis

Purpose: Full diagnostic of confirmed system failure.

Nature: Multi-variable structural breakdown and dependency mapping.

Output:

  • Failure architecture mapping
  • Root structural causes
  • System-level correction direction

Pricing Range: £250–£750+

Pricing Progression Logic

  • Clarity of problem definition
  • Complexity of recurring failure patterns
  • System domain (technical, operational, hybrid)
  • Depth of diagnostic recursion required

As signal quality decreases, diagnostic depth increases.

Boundary Conditions

  • Active or recurring system failure must be present
  • Surface-level optimisation is not sufficient
  • Structural ambiguity must exist in system behaviour

No engagement occurs where no system failure state is identifiable.

Engagement Model

Signal → Classification → Structural Diagnosis → Resolution Pathway
