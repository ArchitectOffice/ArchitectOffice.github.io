
# RD-0 JOURNAL SPECIFICATION

## PURPOSE
Journal entries function as raw cognitive signal capture for downstream transformation into structured RD-0 laws.

---

## STRUCTURE

Each entry MUST include:

TITLE:
Short descriptive label of the observation

TYPE:
Observation | Hypothesis | System Behaviour | Drift | Anomaly

CONTEXT:
Where and how the observation occurred

RAW OBSERVATION:
Unstructured description of event or pattern

INTERPRETATION:
Optional human inference layer

SIGNAL EXTRACTED:
Core structural insight (if identifiable)

CONFIDENCE:
Low | Medium | High

REPEATABILITY:
One-off | Repeated | Stable Pattern

RD-0 TAG:
RD-0E (external system)
RD-0I (internal inference system)
RD-0H (human layer)

LINKED CONCEPTS:
References to SAP-0 or other laws

---

## PROCESSING RULE

No entry may be promoted to:
- Future Law Registry
- Canonical RD-0 Law

without passing through signal extraction + repeatability validation.

---

## SYSTEM ROLE

The journal is not storage.

It is:
> a structured signal intake layer for law generation.
