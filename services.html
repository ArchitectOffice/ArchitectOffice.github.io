Service Architecture

Structured diagnostic layers for AI interaction, decision systems, and operational clarity.

System Overview

Architect Office operates as a structured diagnostic system.

The purpose is not to provide advice or optimisation strategies. The purpose is to identify where structural failure occurs in systems where human intent and machine interpretation diverge.

Core Diagnostic Framework

All services operate through a single structural pipeline:

Signal → Classification → Structural Analysis → Resolution Pathway

This pipeline is used to determine whether instability exists, where it originates, and how it propagates through a system.

RD-0 Rapid Diagnostic

Entry-level structural classification layer

Purpose: Determine whether a real system failure exists.

Function: Initial mapping of ambiguity and instability within the system.

What it produces:

  • Failure / non-failure classification
  • High-level structural fault localisation
  • Identification of system instability zones

RD-0 is not a solution layer. It is a classification layer.

OSD Operational Systems Diagnostic

Deep structural failure analysis layer

Purpose: Full diagnostic analysis of confirmed system failure.

Function: Multi-variable system breakdown mapping and causality tracing.

What it produces:

  • Failure architecture mapping
  • Root structural causes
  • Propagation pathways of instability
  • System-level correction direction

OSD is where system behaviour is translated into structured causality.

Relationship Between Layers

RD-0 identifies whether a structural problem exists.

OSD determines how and why that structural problem exists.

Together, they form a two-stage diagnostic system:

  • Detection (RD-0)
  • Structural Analysis (OSD)

Positioning

Architect Office is not a coaching or optimisation service.

It is a structured diagnostic system for identifying breakdowns in:

  • AI interaction systems
  • decision structures
  • operational workflows
  • interpretation layers between human intent and system response
